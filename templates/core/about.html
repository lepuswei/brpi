About this demonstrator

The Bivariate Reflux Probability Instrument (BRPI) concept treats symptom frequency and intensity as separate predictors, including interaction terms, inside a transparent illustrative logistic equation.

This web application implements Phases 1–2 of the research demonstrator: paper-example reproduction, interactive fictional entry, safety override, contributor explanation, and illustrative plots. It is not a validated clinical prediction model.

  • Model: IllustrativeLogisticModel v1.0.0
  • Validation status: illustrative_only
  • Clinical use permitted: false
