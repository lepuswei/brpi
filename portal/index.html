About MedDemo

Medical innovation often depends on making complex methods understandable: how variables are collected, how they enter a model, and how outputs should not be interpreted. MedDemo provides a lightweight public showcase for such work—starting with gastrointestinal symptom modelling and expanding as new demonstrators are ready.

Each subdirectory hosts an independent application with its own specification, safety language, and release notes. Demonstrators may use fictional or non-identifiable data and illustrative coefficients unless explicitly stated otherwise in an approved research protocol.

Research notice

Not for clinical diagnosis or treatment. MedDemo applications are research and communication artifacts. They must not delay urgent assessment, endoscopy, cardiac evaluation, or any clinically indicated care. Do not rely on illustrative probabilities, zones, or outputs for healthcare decisions unless a tool has undergone formal validation and regulatory assessment for that purpose.

Questions about a specific demonstrator should be directed to the project team cited in its manuscript or specification. MedDemo does not collect personally identifying information in standard demonstration mode unless a separate ethics-approved study is explicitly enabled.
